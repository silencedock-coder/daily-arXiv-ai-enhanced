Motivation: 研究擦除信道下码集合的平均译码错误概率特性，为编码理论提供基础性能界限和分析工具。

Method: 运用组合数学和概率分析方法，推导所有$(n,M)_q$码集合在明确译码(unambiguous decoding)、列表译码(list decoding)和最大似然译码(maximum-likelihood decoding)三种准则下的平均译码错误概率显式公式；进而通过渐近分析得到码率为$R$时的渐近错误指数。

Abstract: In this paper, we provide explicit formulas for the average decoding error probabilities of the ensemble of all $(n,M)_q$ codes over the erasure channel under unambiguous, list and maximum-likelihood decoding principles. Moreover, we derive the asymptotic error exponents for the ensemble of all $(n,q^{Rn})_q$ codes.

### [2] [Analytical Statistics of Vortex Beams in a Turbulent Channel for OAM-Multiplexed FSO Communications](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04700)
*Xinyi Chu,Bo Cao,Renzhi Yuan,Xiangtian Zhao,Haifeng Yao,Mugen Peng*

Main category: eess.SP

TL;DR: 本文研究了大气湍流中涡旋光束的传播特性，推导了接收面辐照度统计量和OAM解复用端口功率统计量的解析模型，并用于评估OAM复用FSO通信的误码率性能。数值结果表明，OAM复用性能对小接收孔径的模式间隔更敏感。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 轨道角动量(OAM)复用可提升自由空间光(FSO)通信容量，但大气湍流会引起模式串扰和辐照度波动，降低解复用性能。因此，需要建立解析模型来表征湍流引起的传播效应以及OAM信道的解复用端口功率统计特性。

Method: 1) 利用频域卷积推导接收面平均辐照度，基于扩展Rytov理论获得闭式频域衍射核，评估中强湍流下的闪烁指数；2) 基于一般LG涡旋场的复高斯展开和扩展Huygens-Fresnel框架，推导平均端口功率、模式串扰、端口功率方差和交叉端口协方差；3) 利用推导的端口功率统计量评估OAM复用FSO通信的误码率(SER)。

Result: 1) 所有推导的统计量与不同湍流强度和光束参数下的相位屏模拟结果一致；2) SER性能表明，OAM复用性能对小接收孔径的模式间隔比大孔径更敏感。

Conclusion: 本文建立了完整的OAM复用FSO通信在大气湍流中的解析模型，涵盖从光束传播统计到系统级性能评估。该模型为OAM-FSO系统的设计和优化提供了理论工具，特别是在模式间隔和接收孔径尺寸的权衡方面具有指导意义。

Abstract: Orbital angular momentum (OAM) multiplexing can increase the capacity of free-space optical (FSO) communications, whereas atmospheric turbulence causes modal crosstalk and irradiance fluctuations that degrade demultiplexing performance. Analytical modeling is therefore important for characterizing turbulence-induced propagation effects and the demultiplexed port-power statistics of OAM channels. In this paper, we first study the receiver-plane irradiance statistics of vortex beams after propagating through the turbulent channel. The average irradiance is derived using frequency-domain convolution, and a closed-form frequency-domain diffraction kernel is obtained based on extended Rytov theory to evaluate the scintillation index for moderate and strong turbulence. However, receiver-plane irradiance statistics alone are insufficient to describe the performance of OAM-multiplexed FSO communications. We therefore derive the demultiplexed port-power statistics. Specifically, we derive the average port power, modal crosstalk, port-power variance, and cross-port covariance based on the complex Gaussian expansion of general LG vortex fields and the extended Huygens-Fresnel framework. The demultiplexed port-power statistics are then used to evaluate the symbol-error rate (SER) of OAM-multiplexed FSO communications. Numerical results demonstrate that all derived statistics are consistent with those obtained by phase-screen simulations under different turbulence strengths and beam parameters. The resulting SER performance further shows that OAM-multiplexing performance is more sensitive to mode spacing for small receiver apertures than for large apertures.

### [3] [AFDM-Enabled ISAC in Dynamic Environments: Fundamentals, Technologies and Opportunities](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04876)
*Linchu Chen,Zhendong Li,Zhou Su,Lin Chen,Wen Chen*

Main category: eess.SP

TL;DR: 本文综述了仿射频分复用（AFDM）波形在动态通感一体化中的应用，指出AFDM通过分离时延-多普勒效应有效应对高移动性挑战，系统梳理了其原理、优势、场景、关键技术及未来方向。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 动态环境对通感一体化（ISAC）构成根本性挑战，主要包括严重的多普勒效应、快速时变信道以及时延-多普勒耦合问题。现有波形难以有效应对这些挑战，亟需能够表征和分离时延与多普勒效应的新型波形方案。

Method: 本文采用综述性研究方法，系统分析了仿射频分复用（AFDM）波形在动态ISAC中的应用潜力。具体包括：（1）刻画动态环境下ISAC的关键特征并介绍AFDM基本原理；（2）分析AFDM能够带来显著性能优势的应用场景；（3）详细阐述AFDM动态ISAC的关键使能技术并结合案例研究；（4）讨论开放挑战与未来研究方向。

Result: AFDM凭借其固有的时延-多普勒表征与分离能力，成为动态ISAC的有前景波形方案。研究明确了AFDM在高移动性场景下的显著性能优势，系统梳理了其关键使能技术，并通过案例研究验证了技术可行性，为该新兴领域的研究者和从业者提供了全面参考。

Conclusion: AFDM使能的动态环境ISAC是一个具有重要应用前景的新兴研究方向。本文通过系统性综述，从基本原理、核心优势、典型场景、关键技术到未来挑战构建了完整的技术框架，为后续创新研究奠定基础并指明方向。

Abstract: Dynamic environments pose fundamental challenges to integrated sensing and communication (ISAC), particularly due to severe Doppler effects, rapidly time-varying channels, and the intricate coupling between delay and Doppler shifts. Affine frequency-division multiplexing (AFDM), with its inherent capability of characterizing and separating delay and Doppler effects, has emerged as a promising waveform for dynamic ISAC. This article provides a comprehensive overview on AFDM-enabled ISAC in dynamic environments, covering its fundamental principles, distinctive advantages, representative application scenarios, and key enabling technologies. We first characterize the key features of ISAC in dynamic environments and introduce the fundamentals of AFDM, followed by an analysis of scenarios where AFDM can provide significant performance benefits. Then, several key enabling technologies for AFDM-based ISAC in dynamic environments are elaborated upon, accompanied by case studies on the critical aspects therein. Finally, open challenges and promising future research directions are discussed, aiming to provide a comprehensive reference for researchers and practitioners while inspiring further innovation in this emerging field.
