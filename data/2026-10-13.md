Method: 1. 将虚拟用户归约技术扩展到异构缓存容量场景，从而从非隐私方案构建需求隐私编码缓存方案；2. 针对两个用户、两个文件的基本设置，提出非对称缓存放置策略，针对异构文件大小和缓存容量进行定制；3. 通过构造显式编码缓存方案实现非平凡角点，再结合内存共享完成可达性证明；4. 利用需求隐私引起的分布等价性推导信息论下界。

Result: 1. 精确刻画了两个用户、两个文件在任意文件大小和缓存容量下的内存-速率权衡；2. 通过八条线性不等式完整表征了最优权衡；3. 推广了同质需求隐私编码缓存的精确权衡结果；4. 量化了与无非隐私异构设置相比，需求隐私所带来的额外传输成本。

Conclusion: 本文首次在异构文件大小和缓存容量条件下，精确刻画了需求隐私编码缓存的内存-速率权衡。核心结论表明：通过扩展虚拟用户技术和设计非对称缓存放置策略，可以实现最优权衡；八条线性不等式完整描述了该权衡边界。该工作将需求隐私编码缓存理论从同质设置推广到异构设置，为实际系统中隐私保护与传输效率的折中提供了精确的理论指导。

Abstract: We consider the demand-private coded caching problem with heterogeneous file sizes and cache capacities, where each user must recover its requested file without learning any information about the demands of the other users. We first extend the virtual-user reduction technique to accommodate heterogeneous cache sizes, thereby enabling the construction of demand-private coded caching schemes from non-private schemes. Our main contribution is an exact characterization of the memory-rate trade-off for the fundamental setting of two users and two files with arbitrary file sizes and cache capacities. Specifically, we characterize the optimal trade-off through eight linear inequalities and establish achievability by constructing explicit coded caching schemes for the non-trivial corner points, followed by memory sharing. These constructions include novel asymmetric cache placement strategies tailored to heterogeneous file sizes and cache capacities. For the converse, we exploit the distributional equivalences induced by demand privacy to derive information-theoretic lower bounds that explicitly account for the underlying heterogeneity. Our results generalize the exact memory-rate trade-off characterization for the homogeneous demand-private coded caching setting with two users and two files, while quantifying the additional transmission cost imposed by demand privacy compared with the non-private heterogeneous setting.
