### [1] [3D Protraction Technique for Anchor-Free, Range-Free Wireless Localization in Industrial Warehouses via Multi-Level Binary Reachability](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09330)
*Peyman Moeini,Mark Coates*

Main category: eess.SP

TL;DR: 本文提出了一种无需锚点和测距的3D定位方法3D-PT，通过多发射功率等级的包检测构建可靠性加权序数距离关系，在模拟仓库环境中相比现有方法显著降低了定位误差。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 工业设施中的三维定位面临挑战，因为固定节点的部署受到硬件成本、供电、网络、安装和维护成本等因素的限制，传统方法依赖RSSI幅度、估计距离、测量锚点或先验坐标，在实际工业环境中难以实施。

Method: 提出三维延伸技术(3D-PT)，一种无锚点、无测距的方法。该方法将有序发射功率等级下的双向包检测转换为可靠性加权的序数距离关系，无需RSSI幅度值、估计距离、测量锚点或先验坐标即可估计相对三维构型。通过多功率等级的二元可达性信息提取几何约束。

Result: 在模拟多高度仓库环境中，使用四个发射功率等级时：(1) 在小规模和中等规模网络中，相对于MDS-MAP(P)方法，平均网络直径归一化RMSE均降低16.0%；(2) 相对于CATL-reproduction方法，在小规模网络中RMSE降低23.9%，中等规模网络中降低34.0%；(3) 超过四个功率等级后，定位精度基本饱和，表明多等级二元可达性相比单一最大功率连通图提供了更有用的几何信息。

Conclusion: 多功率等级的二元可达性信息能够提供超越单一最大功率连通图的有用几何信息，3D-PT方法在无需复杂硬件和先验信息的条件下实现了较好的三维相对定位性能，且四个功率等级即可达到精度饱和，具有良好的实用性和成本效益。

Abstract: Three-dimensional (3D) localization is difficult in industrial facilities because fixed-node placement is constrained by hardware, power, networking, installation, and maintenance costs. This paper presents the 3D Protraction Technique (3D-PT), an anchor-free, range-free method that converts reciprocal packet detections at ordered transmit-power levels into reliability-weighted ordinal distance relations. It estimates a relative 3D configuration without Received Signal Strength Indicator (RSSI) magnitudes, estimated ranges, surveyed anchors, or prior coordinates. In a simulated multi-height warehouse, four transmit-power levels reduce mean network-diameter-normalized RMSE by 16.0\% relative to MDS-MAP(P) in both small- and medium-network regimes, and by 23.9\% and 34.0\% relative to CATL-reproduction, respectively. Accuracy largely saturates beyond four power levels. The results show that multi-level binary reachability provides useful geometric information beyond a single maximum-power connectivity graph.

### [2] [Distributed Physical Layer Authentication and Collaborative RSMA in Non-Terrestrial Networks via Graph Reinforcement Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09475)
*Parsa Rajabi,Mohammad Mirzaee,Mohammad Reza Abedi,Nader Mokari,Paeiz Azmi*

Main category: eess.SP

TL;DR: 本文针对非地面网络（NTN）提出了一种安全自适应联邦认证方案SAFA-MZ，通过将组级认证标签嵌入多层速率分割多址（RSMA）传输结构，结合人工噪声和差分隐私保护，在保证认证可靠性的同时最大化保密频谱效率，并设计了两种可扩展的优化算法。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有NTN物理层认证（PLA）方案存在三个关键缺陷：（1）依赖单锚点验证，缺乏鲁棒性；（2）缺乏认证与传输的联合设计；（3）忽视窃听环境下的标签隐私泄露风险。针对被动、位置已知、静态且无法获取合法信道状态信息（CSI）的窃听者威胁模型，需要一种能够同时保障认证安全性与传输效率的集成方案。

Method: 提出SAFA-MZ方案，核心方法包括：（1）将组级认证标签嵌入协作多层RSMA传输结构；（2）联合波束成形设计私有信号与公共信号；（3）注入人工噪声（AN）降低信息泄露；（4）采用组差分隐私（GDP）防御推理攻击；（5）基于语义优先级对用户分组以按信息重要性分配SSE；（6）联合优化HAPS部署、用户关联和RSMA功率分配；（7）采用基于修复的交叉熵方法（RCEM）和图感知优势演员-评论家算法（GA2C）求解非凸优化问题。

Result: 算法复杂度方面，RCEM随用户数二次方增长，GA2C线性增长且可实现可扩展低延迟推理。仿真结果表明：在合谋与非合谋窃听者场景下，所提方案相比单连接传输平均SSE提升135%，相比无AN方案提升21%，验证了SAFA-MZ在动态NTN环境中的可扩展性和安全性。

Conclusion: SAFA-MZ通过认证-传输联合设计、多层RSMA架构、人工噪声与差分隐私的协同保护，以及语义感知的用户分组策略，为NTN提供了一种可扩展的安全物理层认证解决方案。GA2C算法在保持线性复杂度的同时实现了高效的实时推理，适用于大规模动态NTN部署。

Abstract: Existing physical-layer authentication (PLA) schemes for non-terrestrial networks (NTNs) often rely on single-anchor verification, lack joint authentication-transmission design, and ignore tag privacy leakage under eavesdropping. In this paper, we consider passive, location-aware, static eavesdroppers without access to legitimate channel state information (CSI). Under this threat model, we propose secure adaptive federated authentication for multi-zone NTN systems (SAFA-MZ) that maximizes secrecy spectral efficiency (SSE) while ensuring authentication reliability, power limits, and coverage constraints. The main idea is to embed group-level authentication tags into a collaborative multi-layer rate-splitting multiple access (RSMA) transmission structure. Private and common signals are jointly beamformed, artificial noise (AN) is used to reduce information leakage, and group differential privacy (GDP) protects tag information against inference attacks. In addition, users are grouped by semantic priority to allocate SSE based on information importance. We formulate a joint SSE maximization problem under authentication reliability and probabilistic secrecy constraints, optimizing high-altitude platform station (HAPS) placement, user association, and RSMA power allocation. The resulting problem is solved using a repair-based cross-entropy method (RCEM) and a graph-aware advantage actor-critic algorithm (GA2C). RCEM scales quadratically with the number of users, while GA2C scales linearly and achieves scalable, low-latency inference. Simulation results under both colluding and non-colluding eavesdroppers show that the proposed method improves average SSE by up to 135% over single-connect transmission and 21% over the scheme without AN. These results confirm SAFA-MZ offers a scalable and secure solution for dynamic NTN environments.

### [3] [Shaping Delay-Doppler Ambiguity in Practical OFDM-ISAC](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10300)
*Cheng Luo,Luping Xiang,Kun Yang*

Main category: eess.SP

TL;DR: 针对实际OFDM帧中混合资源结构导致的ROI旁瓣问题，提出深度块酉预编码OFDM(DBU-OFDM)，通过可训练酉变换与专用感知子载波抑制旁瓣，理论证明了相关不变性性质，实验验证可获得数dB至数十dB性能增益。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 正交频分复用(OFDM)是集成感知与通信(ISAC)的关键波形。现有OFDM模糊度分析通常假设完全占用的纯数据波形，但实际帧包含直流和边缘保护空子载波、固定导频和随机有效载荷符号。这种混合资源结构重塑了自模糊函数，并在感兴趣感知区域(ROI)中产生显著的旁瓣，影响感知性能。

Method: 提出面向ROI的深度块酉预编码OFDM(DBU-OFDM)方法：(1) 结合资源特定的可训练酉变换与专用感知子载波；(2) 开发保持约束的参数化方法，能够表示任意酉矩阵；(3) 设计ROI感知感知支持初始化策略；(4) 分别针对周期自相关函数(P-ACF)和非周期自相关函数(A-ACF)模式进行优化。

Result: 理论证明：仅相位优化下P-ACF不变性，任意酉变换下零延迟多普勒切割不变性。数值结果表明：CP-OFDM在P-ACF最优性方面得到验证；传统OFDM在A-ACF设置中可能全局最优。性能增益：仅酉导频优化使ROI峰值旁瓣比提升超过2dB；结合专用感知子载波可获得数dB至数十dB增益；A-ACF模式比P-ACF模式具有更强的ROI模糊度整形能力。

Conclusion: DBU-OFDM通过资源特定的可训练酉变换有效抑制了实际OFDM帧结构在ROI中的旁瓣，同时保持规定的资源支持。所提出的约束保持参数化和ROI感知初始化方法为ISAC波形设计提供了有效框架。A-ACF模式在ROI模糊度整形方面优于P-ACF模式，为实际ISAC系统波形优化提供了重要指导。

Abstract: Orthogonal frequency-division multiplexing (OFDM) is a key waveform for integrated sensing and communication (ISAC). Existing OFDM ambiguity analyses, however, typically assume fully occupied data-only waveforms, whereas practical frames contain direct-current and edge-guard nulls, fixed pilots, and random payload symbols. This mixed resource structure reshapes the self-ambiguity function and induces prominent sidelobes in the sensing region of interest (ROI). We therefore propose ROI-oriented deep block-unitary precoded OFDM (DBU-OFDM), which combines resource-specific trainable unitary transformations with dedicated sensing subcarriers to suppress ROI sidelobes while preserving the prescribed resource support. We develop a constraint-preserving parameterization capable of representing arbitrary unitary matrices and an ROI-aware sensing-support initialization. We prove periodic autocorrelation function (P-ACF) invariance under phase-only optimization and zero-delay Doppler-cut invariance under arbitrary unitary transformations. Numerical results corroborate the P-ACF optimality of cyclic-prefix OFDM (CP-OFDM) and provide numerical support for the conjecture that conventional OFDM is globally optimal in the considered aperiodic autocorrelation function (A-ACF) setting. Unitary pilot optimization improves the ROI peak-to-sidelobe ratio by over 2 dB, while combining it with dedicated sensing subcarriers yields gains ranging from several to tens of dB. Under the considered configuration, the A-ACF mode also provides substantially greater ROI ambiguity-shaping capability than the P-ACF mode.

### [4] [Risk-Averse Decision Making via Quantum Measurement Design](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10482)
*Meiyi Zhu,Osvaldo Simeone*

Main category: cs.IT

TL;DR: 将风险厌恶优化引入量子测量设计，使用优化确定性等价（OCE）框架（含CVaR），对分段线性增益函数转化为半定规划，二态判别得Helstrom形式闭式解，以适度平均效用代价改善下尾部风险。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 传统量子测量优化仅最大化依赖于真实态和测量结果的平均效用函数，但当测量结果被用作更大决策系统中的动作时，平均效用无法捕捉不良结果的风险。因此，需要设计能够最大化风险厌恶目标的量子测量。

Method: 提出使用优化确定性等价（OCE）作为风险厌恶目标函数，该准则族包含平均效用和条件风险价值（CVaR）作为特例。对于定义OCE的分段线性增益函数（包括CVaR），将问题转化为有限个半定规划问题，并导出其对偶形式。对于两个量子态的判别问题，获得了闭式解。

Result: 证明了对于分段线性增益函数（包括CVaR），OCE最大化问题可约化为有限个半定规划；推导了对偶 formulation；对于二态判别，获得了Helstrom测量形式的闭式解；数值结果表明优化测量以适度的平均效用代价改善了效用分布的下尾部。

Conclusion: 该工作将风险厌恶决策理论引入量子测量设计，通过OCE框架实现了对效用分布尾部风险的有效控制，为量子信息处理中的鲁棒决策提供了理论基础。特别地，对于实际重要的CVaR准则和二态判别场景，获得了可计算的高效解决方案。

Abstract: Quantum measurements are conventionally optimized to maximize the average of a utility that depends on the true state and on the measurement outcome. However, when the outcome of the measurement is used as an action within a larger decision-making system, the average utility does not capture the risk of poor outcomes. This letter addresses the design of quantum measurements that maximize a risk-averse objective given by the optimized certainty equivalent (OCE), a family of criteria that includes the average utility and the conditional value at risk (CVaR) as special cases. For a piecewise linear gain function, defining the OCE, thus including the CVaR, the problem is shown to reduce to a finite number of semidefinite programs, for which a dual formulation is derived. For the discrimination of two states, a closed-form solution is obtained that takes the form of a Helstrom measurement. Numerical results show that the optimized measurement improves the lower tail of the utility distribution at a moderate cost in average utility.
