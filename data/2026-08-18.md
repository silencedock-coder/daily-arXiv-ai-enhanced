### [1] [Tensor Decomposition-Based Wireless Sensing for MIMO-OFDM ISAC via Flexible Spatial-Temporal-Spectral Optimization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.15792)
*Chengzhi Ye,Ruoyu Zhang,Lei Yao,Xinrong Guan,Yu Zhang,Wen Wu,Rui Zhang*

Main category: eess.SP

TL;DR: 本文提出了一种灵活空间-时间-频谱优化的MIMO-OFDM ISAC感知框架，利用张量分解实现目标参数并行估计，并通过优化天线位置与时频资源配置最小化CRB，突破了传统固定配置对感知自由度的限制。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有MIMO-OFDM ISAC设计依赖固定位置天线和固定时频资源分配，限制了无线感知在空间-时间-频谱维度上的自由度。需要突破这种固定配置约束，提升感知性能。

Method: 提出一种具有灵活空间-时间-频谱优化的MIMO-OFDM ISAC无线感知框架，基于张量分解方法估计目标参数。具体包括：1) 建立单基地无线感知模型，天线位置、OFDM符号和子载波可灵活配置；2) 将目标参数估计问题建模为规范多面体分解(CP分解)形式的张量分解问题，实现从空间、时间、频谱维度的并行参数估计；3) 推导未知目标参数的克拉美-罗界(CRB)；4) 基于CRB分析，优化天线位置、子载波分配和OFDM符号分配。

Result: 揭示了方位角/俯仰角、速度和距离的估计精度分别由阵列几何结构、OFDM符号分布和子载波分布决定。获得了天线位置、子载波分配和OFDM符号分配的最优解，以最小化CRB和目标参数估计的均方误差。

Conclusion: 通过灵活配置空间-时间-频谱资源并采用张量分解方法，所提出的框架能够显著提升MIMO-OFDM ISAC系统的目标参数估计性能，为6G通信系统中的感知资源优化提供了理论基础和实用方案。

Abstract: Integrated sensing and communication (ISAC) is regarded as a key enabling technique in future 6th-generation (6G) mobile communication systems. However, existing multi-input multi-output (MIMO) orthogonal frequency division multiplexing (OFDM) ISAC designs generally rely on the fixed-position antennas and fixed allocation of time-frequency resources, thereby limiting the degrees of freedom of wireless sensing along the spatial-temporal-spectral dimensions. In this paper, we propose a novel wireless sensing framework for MIMO-OFDM ISAC systems with flexible spatial-temporal-spectral optimization and propose a tensor decomposition-based approach to estimate target parameters, including azimuth/elevation angles, ranges, and velocities. Specifically, we first establish a monostatic wireless sensing model for MIMO-OFDM ISAC systems, where the positions of antenna elements, the allocation of OFDM symbols and subcarriers can be flexibly configured. Then, we formulate the problem of estimating target parameters as a tensor decomposition problem admitting to the canonical polyadic format, which enables the parallel target parameters estimation process from corresponding factor matrices along the spatial, temporal, and spectral dimensions, respectively. Based on the decomposed factor matrices, we derive the Cramer-Rao Bound (CRB) for the unknown target parameters and reveal that the estimation accuracy of azimuth/elevation angles, velocities and ranges is fundamentally determined by the array geometry, the distribution of OFDM symbols and subcarriers. Building on this insight, we obtain an optimized solution for the positions of antenna elements, and optimal solutions for the subcarrier allocation and OFDM symbol allocation to minimize the CRB, as well as the mean square error of target parameters estimation.

### [2] [ISAC in 3GPP: Evolution Toward 6G](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.15283)
*Neeraj Varshney*

Main category: cs.NI

TL;DR: 本文综述了3GPP ISAC从R19到R20的标准化演进轨迹，涵盖物理层到架构层的多维度技术要素，并识别了波形设计、多节点协调、隐私保护等关键开放问题，为6G ISAC的标准化发展提供了系统性参考框架。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 随着6G发展，集成感知与通信（ISAC）成为3GPP演进的重要方向，蜂窝网络需要在提供连接性的同时提供环境感知能力。本文旨在梳理3GPP从Release 19可行性研究到Release 20的无线、协议和架构研究的发展轨迹，为6G ISAC的标准化演进提供系统性解读。

Method: 本文采用综述分析方法，系统梳理3GPP标准演进路径，区分已确立需求、正在进行的假设研究和未来可能方向。调研范围涵盖：服务需求、感知拓扑结构、超越TR 38.901的信道模型演进、RAN1物理层设计、RAN2/RAN3系统层面影响，以及感知辅助通信的作用。同时综合分析了波形与参考信号设计、多节点协调、感知数据上报、服务开放、隐私和实现约束等未解决问题。

Result: 论文建立了从服务层动机到物理层、协议和架构影响的完整映射，提供了以标准为中心的3GPP向实用化6G ISAC支持演进的全景解读。识别了当前标准化进程中的关键开放问题，包括波形与参考信号设计优化、多节点感知协调机制、感知数据的高效上报架构、网络服务开放接口、用户隐私保护以及实际部署约束等核心挑战。

Conclusion: 3GPP ISAC标准化正处于从可行性研究向具体技术方案演进的关键阶段，Release 19至Release 20的过渡标志着该技术从概念验证走向系统设计。未来6G ISAC的成功部署需要在物理层波形设计、网络架构协议优化以及隐私安全保护等多个维度实现协同突破，同时需持续平衡感知性能与通信开销之间的固有权衡。

Abstract: Integrated sensing and communication (ISAC) is emerging as an important direction in the Third Generation Partnership Project (3GPP) evolution toward 6G because it allows cellular networks to provide environmental awareness in addition to connectivity. This paper surveys the current 3GPP trajectory from Release~19 feasibility studies to Release~20 radio, protocol, and architecture studies, while distinguishing established requirements, ongoing study assumptions, and possible forward directions. The survey covers service requirements, sensing topologies, channel model evolution beyond 3GPP Technical Report (TR)~38.901, Radio Access Network Working Group~1 (RAN1) physical layer design, Radio Access Network Working Groups~2 and~3 (RAN2 and RAN3) system implications, and the role of sensing-assisted communication. It also synthesizes the main unresolved issues in waveform and reference signal design, multi-node coordination, sensing data reporting, service exposure, privacy, and implementation constraints. By connecting service-level motivations to physical layer, protocol, and architecture implications, the paper provides a standards-centric reading of how 3GPP may evolve toward practical 6G ISAC support.
