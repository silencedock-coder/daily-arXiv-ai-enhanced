### [1] [Selective Interference Suppression of Siamese-Net in Heterogeneous Interference Channels](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27635)
*Arkadeep Sinha,Shubham Paul,R. Manivasakan,Nambi Seshadri,R. David Koilpillai*

Main category: cs.IT

TL;DR: 本文提出Siamese网络用于学习异构干扰信道中的短块码，发现网络能自动实现选择性干扰抑制——对强干扰用户对强制正交化，对弱干扰用户对保持编码对齐，从而在编码增益与BLER间获得最优权衡。

Motivation: 本文研究在N用户实高斯干扰信道中，针对异构成对干扰强度的端到端学习短块码设计，且每个接收端保持单用户解码。核心动机是探究当仅存在少数主导干扰源时，Siamese式耦合训练是否能够选择性地自适应编码与解码，以实现正交性与编码增益之间的最优权衡，而非盲目地强制全局正交性。

Method: 采用Siamese网络耦合训练框架，针对4用户非对称干扰配置进行端到端学习。具体设置包括：一对主导干扰用户(1,2)具有强度Λ，以及一对弱耦合用户(强度λ)。通过潜在空间(latent space)中的跨用户相似性统计量（包括最坏情况相干度量、平均相似性度量等）来量化学习码本的几何特性。

Result: 实验观察到选择性干扰抑制现象：学习得到的码本对主导干扰用户对呈现近似正交特性，而弱耦合用户对保持对齐以获取编码增益。Siamese网络能够根据干扰强度差异，选择性地抑制不同干扰用户对的干扰，在编码增益与BLER（块误码率）之间实现由正交性决定的最优权衡。这种几何特征与不等干扰条件下的BLER鲁棒性存在明确关联。

Conclusion: Siamese式耦合训练能够智能地适应信道干扰结构，无需显式设计即可实现干扰感知的码本优化。该方法突破了传统全局正交化的局限，为异构干扰环境下的端到端学习编码提供了有效的解决方案，在保持编码增益的同时显著提升了抗干扰性能。

Abstract: We study an end-to-end learnt short-block codes for a $N$-user real Gaussian interference channel with heterogeneous pairwise interference strengths, while keeping single-user decoding at every receiver. In this paper, we study the case wherein only a few dominant interferers exist and investigate whether Siamese-style coupled training can adapt selectively to encode (\& decode) to ensure optimal performance corresponding to best tradeoff between orthogonality and coding gain or it enforces unnecessary global orthogonality oblivious of the reality. Our work focuses on a 4-user unequal-interference configuration with one dominant pair $(1,2)$ (of strength $Λ$) and a weak pair (with weak background coupling ($λ$)), through which we demonstrate a selective interference suppression phenomenon where the learned codebooks become near-orthogonal primarily for the dominant pair, while weakly coupled pairs retain alignment needed for coding gain. We quantify this behaviour using latent-space cross-user similarity statistics (worst-case coherence measure, average similarity measure, etc) and connect these geometric signatures to the observed BLER robustness under unequal interference. It seems that the SiameseNet selectively suppresses the interferences from various interferring user pairs to yield optimal tradeoff between coding gain and BLER dictated by orthogonality.
