### [1] [NDT Factory: Synthesizing Verified Network Digital Twins from Semantic Models via Multi-Agent LLM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.12170)
*Sudipta Acharya,Petar Djukic,Burak Kantarci*

Main category: cs.NI

TL;DR: 本文提出NDT工厂——一个基于大语言模型的多智能体系统，能够按需从语义模型自动合成可执行的行为网络数字孪生（NDT），并通过呼叫准入控制案例验证了其100%编译通过率和99.3%决策一致性，实现了无需预定义分析逻辑的自主网络管理。

Motivation: 自主网络管理（TM Forum L4级自治）需要系统能够在不同条件下自动评估网络服务意图（NSI），而无需手动实现分析逻辑。现有行为网络数字孪生（NDT）依赖预定义的分析逻辑，这限制了其在不断演进的闭环控制中的适应性。

Method: 提出NDT工厂——一个多智能体软件系统，利用大语言模型（LLM）从语义模型按需合成可执行的行为NDT。采用并行合成与编排机制生成完整的呼叫准入控制（CAC）NDT，通过确定性what-if分析作为准入决策过程。

Abstract: Autonomous network management requires systems that can evaluate Network Service Intents (NSIs) under varying conditions without manual implementation of analysis logic, as envisioned in TM Forum Level~4 (L4) autonomy. Behavioral Network Digital Twins (NDTs) enable such evaluation, but existing NDTs rely on pre-defined analytical logic, limiting adaptability for evolving closed-loop control. This paper introduces the NDT factory, a multi-agent software system that synthesizes executable behavioral NDTs on demand from semantic models using Large Language Model (LLM). We validate the system using a Call Admission Control (CAC) case study, where deterministic what-if analysis serves as the admission decision process. The NDT factory generates a complete CAC NDT through parallel synthesis and orchestration, achieving 100% compilation and test pass rates across multiple runs. Simulation over 300 NSIs shows 99.3% decision agreement with a reference implementation, 90% admission rate, and correct attribution of all rejections, demonstrating reliable synthesis with deterministic, verifiable execution.

### [2] [Feedback Capacity of Stationary Gaussian Channels: An Optimal Schalkwijk-Kailath Scheme](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.12069)
*David Fay,Oron Sabag*

Main category: cs.IT

TL;DR: 本文证明了Kim关于反馈容量中反馈独立高斯分量可去除的断言对于具有有理功率谱密度的平稳高斯噪声信道成立，并基于该结果构造了具有双指数衰减最大错误概率的显式SK编码方案。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: Kim的开创性工作给出了反馈容量的平稳变分刻画，并声称容量可达的平稳输入不需要包含反馈独立的高斯分量，进而基于Schalkwijk-Kailath (SK) 细化原理构造了简单的容量可达编码方案。然而，近期一篇笔记指出了反馈独立分量去除断言证明中的一个漏洞，使得SK方案及其后续依赖结果的最优性证明不完整。

Method: 本文采用基于反馈容量凸优化公式的扰动分析方法，证明了对于具有有理功率谱密度的平稳高斯噪声信道，反馈独立分量去除断言成立。具体而言，证明了每个最优解都将零功率分配给反馈独立分量。

Result: 证明了对于具有有理功率谱密度的平稳高斯噪声信道，每个反馈容量优化器都将零功率分配给反馈独立分量。基于这一更强的性质，从任意优化器构造了显式的SK编码方案，该方案能够以低于反馈容量的任意速率实现通信，且具有双指数衰减的最大错误概率。

Conclusion: 本文填补了Kim工作中关于反馈独立高斯分量去除断言的证明漏洞，为SK编码方案的最优性提供了严格的理论基础，并构造了具有双指数衰减错误概率的显式容量可达编码方案。

Abstract: We consider channels with additive colored Gaussian noise and noiseless feedback. Kim's seminal work derived a stationary variational characterization of feedback capacity and further asserted that the capacity-achieving stationary input need not contain a feedback-independent Gaussian component. These results led to the construction of a simple coding scheme, based on the Schalkwijk--Kailath (SK) refinement principle, which was shown to be capacity-achieving. A recent note identified a gap in the proof of the feedback-independent component-removal assertion, thereby leaving the optimality of the SK scheme and subsequent results that rely on it incomplete. In this paper, we prove the component-removal assertion for channels with stationary Gaussian noise that has a rational power spectral density. Our proof uses a perturbation analysis of a convex optimization formulation of feedback capacity and, indeed, shows that every optimizer assigns zero power to the feedback-independent component. Using this stronger property, we construct from any optimizer an explicit SK coding scheme that achieves every rate below feedback capacity with doubly-exponentially decaying maximal error probability.

### [3] [Joint Random Access and Localization in Cell-Free User-Centric Networks with Frequency-Selective Fading Channels](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.12140)
*Simon Tarboush,Eleni Gkiouzepi,Giuseppe Caire*

Main category: cs.IT

TL;DR: 本文针对无蜂窝用户中心网络中的随机接入（RACH）问题，提出了一种统一的联合检测与定位框架，考虑了帧同步但码片异步传输、视距传播以及多径非视距传播等因素。通过对比传统的Zadoff-Chu序列方案和基于多源近似消息传递（AMP）的方法，数值结果表明AMP方案在前导检测方面表现更优，而两种方案在定位能力上均表现优异。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 无蜂窝用户中心网络需要处理大量地理分布广泛、具有偶发业务和间歇性活动的用户。随机接入过程必须实现两个关键功能：1）检测随机接入时隙中（尚未知的）随机接入用户发送的前导码；2）对用户进行定位以快速分配用户中心无线单元（RU）簇。现有工作大多使用简化模型，忽略了帧同步但码片异步传输、某些用户-RU对之间可能存在的视距（LoS）传播，以及产生频率选择性信道的多径非视距（NLoS）传播。因此，需要建立一个更真实、空间一致的信道模型下的统一联合检测与定位框架。

Method: 1. 采用基于地理位置的分区随机接入码本：不同地理区域（位置）的用户使用对应的随机接入前导码子集。
2. 建立统一的联合检测与定位框架：基于空间一致的网络级信道模型，整合帧同步但码片异步传输、LoS传播和多径NLoS频率选择性衰落等被忽略的因素。
3. 评估两种方案：
   - "传统"方案：使用Zadoff-Chu（ZC）序列，将3GPP两步RACH扩展到无蜂窝场景；
   - 多源近似消息传递（AMP）方案：扩展到多径频率选择性衰落场景。
4. 为两种方案开发了近似的广义似然比检验（GLRT）前导检测器和最大似然（ML）位置估计器，并带有超分辨率细化，隐式利用嵌入LoS分量中的接收信号强度、到达角度和到达时间差信息。

Result: 1. 基于AMP的方案在前导检测方面实现了更优的性能；
2. 两种方案在定位能力上表现相似且均十分出色；
3. 所提出的近似GLRT检测器和ML位置估计器能够有效利用LoS分量中的多维度信息（RSS、AOA、TDOA）进行超分辨率定位。

Conclusion: 本文提出的统一框架成功地将实际信道特性（异步传输、LoS/NLoS多径）纳入无蜂窝用户中心网络的随机接入设计中。基于AMP的方案在前导检测性能上优于传统ZC序列方案，而两种方案在定位精度上均能达到优异表现。该研究为未来无蜂窝网络中的高效随机接入和资源分配提供了重要理论基础和技术方案。

Abstract: We study random access (RACH) schemes for cell-free (CF) user-centric networks to handle many geographically distributed users with sporadic traffic and intermittent activity. The RACH must allow the system to: 1) detect preambles sent by the (yet unknown) random access users in the RACH slot; 2) localize them for fast allocation of user-centric radio-unit (RU) clusters. Most prior work uses simplified models, neglecting frame-synchronous but chip-asynchronous transmission, possible line-of-sight (LoS) propagation for certain user-RU pairs, and multipath non-line-of-sight (NLoS) propagation yielding frequency-selective channels. Building on our previous work, we consider location-dependent partitioned random access codebooks where users in a geographic area (location) use the corresponding subset of random access preambles. We present a unified framework for joint detection and localization over a spatially consistent network-wide channel model, incorporating these neglected aspects. We evaluate two schemes: 1) a ``legacy'' scheme using Zadoff-Chu (ZC) sequences, extending the 3GPP 2-step RACH to the CF case; 2) our multisource approximate message passing (AMP) approach extended to multipath frequency-selective fading. For both schemes, we develop novel approximated GLRT preamble detection and Maximum-Likelihood position estimators with super-resolution refinement, implicitly exploiting received signal strength, angle of arrival, and time-difference of arrival information embedded into LoS components. Numerical results show that the AMP-based scheme achieves superior preamble detection, while both schemes have similar and excellent localization capability.

### [4] [Error-Rate Reduction in LDPC Decoding via Bit-Aligned Temporal Reinforcement in Parallel Probabilistic-Bit Dynamics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.12389)
*Naoya Onizawa,Takahiro Hanyu*

Main category: cs.IT

TL;DR: 该研究提出了一种基于饱和响应存储和重用的p-bit退火方法，用于LDPC码解码，相比传统无记忆概率模拟退火，在多个码长下实现了33.5%-81.8%的误码率降低，证明了时序状态中保留量及其重新注入随机动力学对计算效果的关键影响。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 概率比特(p-bits)作为随机推断的物理和算法原语，其高度并行更新会改变集体动力学特性。现有基于p-bit的LDPC码解码方法（如概率模拟退火pSA）存在记忆缺失问题，需要探索如何利用时序状态信息来改善解码性能，特别是在并行更新条件下维持稳定的集体动力学行为。

Method: 1. 研究对象：随机正则(3,6)低密度奇偶校验(LDPC)码的解码问题；2. 核心方法：Jacobi型p-bit退火结合随机部分激活；3. 关键技术：加法响应路径规则——存储每个比特的饱和响应并在随机读出前重用；4. 对比方法：静态增益、归一化平均、响应重排、同比特二元状态反馈等非记忆性方法；5. 实验设计：对不同码长(96, 192, 288)进行高统计量模拟，独立参数优化，30个独立码实现验证

Result: 1. 误码率改善：相比无记忆概率模拟退火(pSA)，码长96、192、288时误码率分别降低33.5%、74.8%、81.8%；2. 消融实验：静态增益、归一化平均、响应重排、同比特二元状态反馈等方法无法完全复现饱和响应的完整收益；3. 机理分析：轨迹分析表明改进源于获得信道一致的有效码字吸引盆以及增强的后获取稳定性；4. 泛化性：获取优势在随机初始化和信道硬判决初始化条件下均持续存在；5. 鲁棒性：固定加法参数-读出包在30个独立码实现中均优于单独优化的pSA专用包，且无需针对单个码重新调参

Conclusion: 时序状态的计算效应取决于保留的数量及其重新注入随机动力学的方式。饱和响应的存储和重用通过促进信道一致码字吸引盆的获取和增强后获取稳定性，显著提升了p-bit退火在LDPC解码中的性能。这一发现为设计高效的随机推断算法和物理实现提供了重要指导，表明适当的记忆机制可以克服并行更新带来的动力学扰动问题。

Abstract: Probabilistic bits (p-bits) provide a physical and algorithmic primitive for stochastic inference, but highly parallel updates can alter their collective dynamics. We study the decoding of random-regular (3, 6) low-density parity-check (LDPC) codes using Jacobi-type p-bit annealing with stochastic partial activation. An additive response-path rule stores each bit's saturated response and reuses it before stochastic readout. High-statistics simulations with independent parameter optimization for each method show pooled bit-error-rate reductions of 33.5%, 74.8%, and 81.8% relative to memoryless probabilistic simulated annealing (pSA) for representative codes of block lengths 96, 192, and 288, respectively. Static gain, normalized averaging, response shuffling, and same-bit binary-state feedback with only its coefficient tuned under the same nonmemory parameters do not reproduce the full saturated-response benefit. Trajectory analysis links the improvement to acquisition of the channel-consistent valid-codeword basin and enhanced post-acquisition stability; the acquisition advantage persists from random and channel-hard-decision starts under the tested conditions. Across all 30 independent code realizations, fixed additive parameter-and-readout packages achieve lower bit- and frame-error rates than separately optimized pSA-specific packages, with neither package retuned for individual codes. These results show that the computational effect of temporal state depends on the retained quantity and its reinjection into stochastic dynamics.
